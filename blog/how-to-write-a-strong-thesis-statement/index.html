How to Write a Strong Thesis Statement

A strong thesis gives your essay a clear direction. Here is how to write one that your body paragraphs can support.

By the Rosjos team November 17, 2026 5 min read

A thesis statement tells the reader what your essay argues and why it matters. When it's weak, the rest of the essay tends to drift. When it's strong, every paragraph has a clear job to do. The qualities of a strong thesis are easy to check once you know what to look for.

Make it arguable

A thesis needs to be a claim that a reasonable person could disagree with. A fact, such as “Many teenagers use social media,” isn't a thesis, because no one would argue with it. A claim such as “Social media does more harm than good for teenagers” can be argued, and your essay can support it with evidence.

Make it specific

Vague theses are hard to support. “Social media has many effects on teenagers” says almost nothing. A stronger version names the effect and the cause: “Social media increases anxiety among teenagers mainly because it encourages constant comparison and interrupts sleep.” Now the reader knows what you will prove and how.

Preview your reasons

A good thesis often hints at the structure of the essay. In the example above, the reasons are comparison and sleep, so the body paragraphs will likely cover those two ideas in that order. This gives the reader a roadmap and gives you a plan for writing.

Check your thesis

  • Could a thoughtful person reasonably disagree with this?
  • Can I support it within the length of my essay, without stretching it?
  • Does each body paragraph connect back to the thesis?
  • Is it one sentence that states my main point clearly?

Revise after you draft

Many writers don't know their exact argument until they've drafted it, and that's normal. Write your first draft, then reread the body paragraphs and ask what they actually show. Often you'll find a sharper thesis hiding in what you already wrote. Update the thesis to match the essay you really wrote, and make sure the rest of the essay follows it.
