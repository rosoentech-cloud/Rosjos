FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

What subjects do you cover?

We cover high school, college, test prep, and writing support. Send us your subject and we'll confirm whether we can help.

How do I get started?

Message us with your subject, your deadline, and what you'd like to work on. We'll confirm the scope and price before you pay.

No. We explain concepts, work through examples, and review drafts. You complete and submit your own work.
