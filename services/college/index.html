What we cover

Built around your syllabus

College courses move quickly, and it's easy to fall behind on a single concept. We use your syllabus, lecture notes, and problem sets as the starting point, so every session connects directly to your course.

Whether you need help before an exam or a steady weekly rhythm, we focus on understanding the material well enough to apply it on your own.

  • Introductory courses in math, science, economics, and the social sciences
  • Upper-level topics that need extra explanation and worked examples
  • Problem set and lab report review, with feedback on your reasoning
  • Exam preparation with practice questions and review of missed answers
How a session works

Three steps from first message to first session

No long forms or commitments. We agree on the plan together before you book.

1

Send your syllabus or course outline

2

3

FAQ

Questions about college course tutoring

Do you tutor courses at my university?

We tutor by subject, not by school. Send us your course name and we'll confirm whether we can help with the material.

Can you help with graded assignments?

We help you understand the material and review your reasoning. Your submitted work should always be your own, and we'll help you build the skills to do it well.

What if my course is very advanced?

Share the course description and what you're struggling with. We'll confirm whether we're a good fit before you book.
