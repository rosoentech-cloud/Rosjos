What we cover

Support across your high school subjects

Every session starts with what you're dealing with right now: your current unit, your next assignment, or a test on the calendar. We then focus on the concepts that will make the biggest difference this week.

We explain ideas in plain language, work through examples together, and check your understanding with practice questions. By the end of a session, you know what to do next and why.

  • Math, from algebra and geometry to precalculus
  • Science, including biology, chemistry, and physics
  • English, including reading comprehension and essay structure
  • Social studies, including sources, arguments, and writing
How a session works

Three steps from first message to first session

No long forms or commitments. We agree on the plan together before you book.

1

Share your class and deadline

Tell us the course, the topic, and when your next assignment or test is due.

2

Build a short study plan

We agree on what to cover first and how many sessions you need to get there.

3

Practice and review

We work through problems or drafts together, then you leave with a clear list of next steps.

FAQ

Questions about high school tutoring

Can you help with homework?

Yes. We help you understand the problem and the method. You complete the assignment yourself so you're ready for the next one.

How long is a session?

Session length depends on your plan. We agree on timing and frequency before you book.

What if my subject isn't listed?

Tell us what you need. If it's not something we cover, we'll say so and point you in a better direction.
