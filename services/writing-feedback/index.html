What we cover

Feedback that teaches, not rewrites

Good feedback should make your next draft better, not just this one. We read your work closely and point out what's working, what isn't, and why, so you can apply the same thinking to future writing.

We don't write your assignments. Instead, we show you how to organize an argument, tighten your sentences, and cite sources correctly.

  • Thesis and argument structure
  • Paragraph organization and transitions
  • Clarity, concision, and tone
  • Citation style and source use in APA, MLA, and Chicago

FAQ

Questions about academic writing feedback

Will you rewrite my essay?

No. We give feedback and explain it, so the writing and the ideas stay yours.

Do you check for plagiarism?

We focus on teaching good citation and source use. For originality checks, please use the tools your institution provides.

What should I send with my draft?

Send your draft, the assignment instructions, the citation style, and any deadlines.
